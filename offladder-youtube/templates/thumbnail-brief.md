## Fixed rules
- Orange `#FA5608` is the only added accent. Ink `#0C0C0C`, paper `#F4F2EE`.
- Archivo Black, uppercase, for all words. Square corners, hard ink offset shadow.
- The series badge goes top-left. Keep the bottom-right corner clear, because YouTube overlays the duration there.
- A real expression from the shoot. No manufactured shock faces.
- Words must add something the title doesn't say.
- 1280×720 (16:9).
