## People
- [ ] A signed release from everyone identifiable on camera (release log updated)
- [ ] Practitioner fee agreed in writing, and it doesn't depend on the outcome
- [ ] Practitioner told they review for **accuracy and safety only**, not editorial control
- [ ] Employer permission in writing (filming at work, or discussing work processes)
- [ ] **No minors on camera.** If an exception is agreed: written parental consent, no school or location details, and a moderation plan
- [ ] Change One Thing: consent, money-disclosure level, family boundaries and wellbeing plan agreed

## Places
- [ ] Written location permission for every non-public location
- [ ] Venue safety induction done, and noted on camera where relevant
- [ ] Screens, documents, badges, client, patient and pupil details: blur plan agreed

## Activities
- [ ] Risky activity (drones, tools, batteries, mains power, heights, labs) led by a qualified practitioner, following local rules
- [ ] Drone: a licensed operator flies, under local aviation rules; landowner permission obtained
- [ ] Viewer experiment (Your Version) checked as safe for a 13-year-old

## Content
- [ ] Planned elements (curveballs, practice briefs, demonstration images) have an on-screen label written into the script
- [ ] Synthetic media: made by us, labelled on screen, and the YouTube disclosure is on the publish checklist
- [ ] No OffLadder user data used anywhere in the idea, script or casting
