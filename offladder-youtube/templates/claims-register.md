## Rules

1. **Primary sources only** for numbers: the organisation that produced the data (for example the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, ILO, OECD, WEF, a national careers service), not an article summarising it.
2. **Pay** appears only as a range, with source and date on an Evidence Card, and never in a title or thumbnail.
3. **Practitioners' experience** is attributed to them ("in [Name]'s work…"), never presented as a statistic.
4. **Host anecdotes** marked **[HOST: real]** are confirmed as true by the host in writing.
5. **Predictions** are reworded as uncertainty ("nobody knows yet whether…") or cut.
6. **Our own research** (for example the list count in script 03): the method goes on screen, and the data is linked in the description.
7. **Every source** used on screen also goes in the description's EVIDENCE block.
