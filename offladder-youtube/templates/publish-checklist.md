# Publish checklist

*Stage 7. Run it for every long-form video. A shorter Shorts section is at the end.*

**Video:** [##] [title] · **Publish time:** · **Checked by:**

## Packaging
- [ ] Final title is **true of the final edit** (outcome-dependent titles resolved)
- [ ] Three thumbnail variants uploaded to Test & Compare, with the preferred one uploaded first
- [ ] Thumbnail QA passed ([template](thumbnail-brief.md))

## Video
- [ ] Hook confirms the thumbnail in the first frame
- [ ] Corrected captions uploaded
- [ ] Evidence Cards on screen for every sourced claim
- [ ] Planned elements labelled on screen (curveballs, practice briefs, demonstration images)
- [ ] Altered or synthetic content disclosure ticked, if any realistic AI-generated or altered media is shown

## Description
- [ ] First two lines: the promise, and a UTM-tagged `/start` link ([05-ctas.md](../05-ctas.md#9-tracking-links))
- [ ] Your Version steps
- [ ] Chapters: 00:00 first, at least three, each at least 10 seconds, non-spoiler titles for experiments
- [ ] EVIDENCE block lists every source
- [ ] Practitioner credit and link
- [ ] Links to the page for parents and the safe-use page (teen-facing videos)
- [ ] "Made by the team behind OffLadder" line

## On the watch page
- [ ] Pinned comment posted (Your Version and prompt)
- [ ] End screen: the specific next video, the series playlist and subscribe, in the last 20 seconds
- [ ] Cards: at most two, never during the verdict
- [ ] Added to its series playlist and to any topic playlists
- [ ] Audience setting: not made for kids (as agreed at launch)
- [ ] Comment moderation on (hold potentially inappropriate comments)

## After publishing
- [ ] Reply to comments for the first hour
- [ ] Shorts from this video scheduled, each with this video as its related video
- [ ] 48-hour review booked in the calendar

## Shorts
- [ ] First-frame text inside the safe area
- [ ] Captions burned in
- [ ] Loop checked (the last line leads into the first frame)
- [ ] Related video attached
- [ ] Comment prompt in on-screen text
- [ ] Synthetic content disclosure ticked, if applicable
