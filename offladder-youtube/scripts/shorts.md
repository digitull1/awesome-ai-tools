# Shorts: twelve scripts

Twelve scripts across the six Shorts series in the slate ([06-video-slate.md](../06-video-slate.md#shorts-slate-24)). Each one is built on the Shorts Loop structure ([04-story-and-retention.md](../04-story-and-retention.md#structure-6-the-shorts-loop)).

## Rules for every Short

- **Vertical 9:16**, 20–60 seconds. Shorts can run to three minutes, but these formats work best short.
- **The first frame is the thumbnail and the hook.** Put 2–6 words of text in it, inside the safe area (clear of the bottom ~20% and the right-hand ~15%).
- **Burn in captions**, in the brand style: Archivo Black, ink on paper, or white on dark footage.
- **Loop the ending**, so the last line leads naturally back into the first frame.
- **Attach the related long-form video.** Links in Shorts descriptions and comments aren't clickable, so the related-video link is the only CTA that works ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639)).
- **Comment prompts go in on-screen text**, not voiceover, so the loop isn't broken.
- **Everyone on camera is an adult with a signed release.** Street interviews: adults only, and ask before filming.

## S01 · Delete One Part: practitioner

**Length:** ~30 s · **Related video:** the practitioner's long-form episode (for example 13)
**Shorts title:** *I asked a [job] which part of their job they'd delete*
**First frame text:** **WHICH PART WOULD YOU DELETE?**

| Time | Picture | Audio | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | The practitioner, framed tight, at their workplace | HOST (off camera): "Which part of your job would you delete if you could?" | WHICH PART WOULD YOU DELETE? |
| 2–15 s | Their answer, uncut if it's good | PRACTITIONER (capture) | Their job title, small |
| 15–18 s | A quick cutaway to what they're describing, if there is one | Location sound | — |
| 18–26 s | Back on them | HOST: "And would you still do the job?" PRACTITIONER (capture) | — |
| 26–30 s | Hold on their face for a beat after the answer | — | *Your job: which part would you delete?* |

**Loop:** their last word cuts straight back to the question in frame one.
**Why it works:** an honest, specific answer from a real person, and a question every working adult wants to answer in the comments.
