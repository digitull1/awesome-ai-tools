# 05 · Calls to action

> The best CTA is the next step of the method. The viewer just watched an experiment, so the natural next move is to run one. OffLadder is the fastest way to pick it.

---

## 1. Principles

1. **Value before the ask.** Every video gives away a usable experiment (Your Version) *before* mentioning the product.
2. **One integrated moment, one closing CTA.** No more than two product mentions per long-form video.
3. **Say plainly that OffLadder is ours.** "The app we make" or "our tool", never a neutral-sounding review. Viewers trust a brand that is open about itself, and the channel shouldn't imply independence it doesn't have.
4. **Describe the product honestly.** It suggests directions to test and learns from what you try. It does not choose your career.
5. **Never mention prices on camera.** Founding prices change, and "free to start, no account" is both true and lower-friction.
6. **Assume a 13-year-old is listening.** Every CTA must be safe and appropriate for them to act on.

---

## 2. The CTA ladder

| CTA | Purpose | When | What we measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Try This** (Your Version) | Give the viewer a real experiment to run this week | Every video, in the final segment | Comments that report back; pinned-comment engagement |
| **Comment** | Engagement, and a feedback loop that generates ideas | Every video, **before** the payoff | Comments per 1,000 views |
| **Product** | Three-question starts on offladder.com | After the payoff; strongest in Honest Answers | UTM sessions → three-question starts |
| **Subscribe** | Turn a viewer into a returning viewer | Once, after the first payoff (40–60% of runtime) or in the bridge | Subscribers per 1,000 views |
| **Binge** | Keep the session going | The last 20 seconds, over the end screen | End-screen element click rate |
| **Casting** | Find real people for Change One Thing | Occasionally, in career-change videos | Applications |

## 3. Where CTAs go, by series

| Series | Integrated product moment | Comment prompt | Subscribe | Closing CTA | Bridge |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **90 Minutes As…** | Beat 2: host shows their real three-question result and picks the direction | Prediction prompt at beat 3 (*"comment your verdict before you see mine"*) | After the curveball resolves | Your Version → *"or get your own two directions"* | Next 90 Minutes |
| **Last Tuesday** | None mid-video | *"Which part of your job would you delete?"* after the hook payoff | After the worst-week segment | Your Version → product line | Matching 90 Minutes, or next Last Tuesday |
| **The Job Nobody Told You About** | Optional: *"this is one of the directions OffLadder can suggest"* | *"Would you try this? Yes, no, or 'depends on…'"* | After "what it grows out of" | One small test → product line | Next unknown job |
| **Change One Thing** | Week 1: the subject answers the three questions (only if they genuinely used it) | *"What would you decide in their position?"* before week 6 | After week 5 | The six-week template → product line → casting | Next person |
| **Honest Answers** | None until the end | A participation beat that doubles as a comment prompt | After the second method step | **The strongest product CTA** (the viewer's question is the one the product answers) | Next logical question |
| **Shorts** | Rarely (at most one Short in four) | On-screen text | Never spoken | The **related video** link to the long-form | The loop |

## 4. Line bank

### Product lines (say these nearly word for word; they are accurate)

**Honest Answers (end):**
> *"If you'd rather have a couple of directions picked for you, plus one small thing to try this week, that's what we built OffLadder for. It's three questions at offladder.com. It's free, there's no account, and it takes about two minutes."*

**90 Minutes As… (integrated, beat 2):**
> *"I didn't pick today's job. I answered three questions on OffLadder, the app we make, and it gave me two directions to test. This was one of them. Your answers will give you different ones. That's the point."*

**90 Minutes As… (end):**
> *"You don't have to test live events. If you want your own two directions and a first experiment, it's three questions at offladder.com. Free, no account."*

**When mentioning Laddie:**
> *"If you get stuck halfway through, you can tell Laddie, the AI guide inside OffLadder, and it'll help you shrink the experiment. It's an AI, so check anything important."*

**Teen-facing videos:**
> *"It's built for thirteen and up, and you don't need an account to see your first directions. If your parents want to know what it is, there's a one-minute page for them too."*

**Honesty line** (use whenever the product is shown giving advice):
> *"It's a starting point to test, not a verdict. Same as everything on this channel."*

### Comment prompts

- *"Before you see my verdict: comment MORE, VARIANT or OFF THE TABLE."*
- *"Which part of your job would you delete? I'll go first in the pinned comment."*
- *"Comment your hobby. We'll reply to as many as we can with the verb underneath it and a job it points to."*
- *"What did a career test once tell you to be? Best answer gets pinned."*
- *"Which job should we test next? We read every suggestion, and the most-liked ones go on the shortlist."*
- *"What would you decide in their position, and why?"*

Only promise what the team can deliver. If we say we'll reply, we reply.

### Subscribe lines (tied to the channel promise, once per video)

- *"We test one real job a week, honestly. If you want to see which one's next, subscribe."*
- *"Every week, one small experiment you can steal. That's the whole channel."*

### Bridge lines (the last 20 seconds)

A spoken bridge must name a video that is **already live on publish day**. The audio can't be changed after upload, and the end screen can only link to published videos.

- *"If you enjoyed watching me panic about a festival, this one's worse: I had ninety minutes to make an AI fail on purpose. It's right here."*
- *"If this was your question, the next video is the one to watch: how to test a direction without quitting anything. It's right here."*

### Subscribe teasers (optional, before the bridge)

- *"Next week, a paramedic tells us about the worst week of their year. Subscribe so you don't miss it."* **[Only if that episode is genuinely scheduled]**

### Casting lines (career-change videos)

> *"If you're thinking about changing direction and you'd be up for testing it on camera for six weeks, the application's linked in the description. You won't have to quit anything. That's the point."*

## 5. On-screen CTA design

- **Lower third:** an orange (`#FA5608`) box, Archivo Black, ink text, hard ink shadow: **OFFLADDER.COM · 3 QUESTIONS · FREE**. On screen for 4–6 seconds.
- **Placement:** lower left, clear of faces, captions and YouTube's player controls.
- **Your Version card:** a paper-coloured card listing the experiment in three numbered lines, on screen long enough to read twice.
- **Scorecard graphic:** the three questions as a checklist, with each one ticking as the host answers.

## 8. End screens and cards

YouTube's rules: a video must be at least 25 seconds long to have an end screen, end screens use the last 5–20 seconds, and a standard 16:9 video can show up to four elements. End screens aren't available on videos set as made for kids ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6388789)).

**Our standard end screen (last 20 seconds):**

1. **Specific video:** the exact video named in the bridge line.
2. **Playlist:** the series playlist.
3. **Subscribe element.**

**Edit for it.** Leave the last 20 seconds of every edit visually clean on one side, with the host still talking (the bridge line) so the screen doesn't feel dead.

**Cards:** at most two per video, pointing to the Honest Answer that explains a method in full (for example, "the whole test-a-career method is in this video"). Never use a card during the verdict.

## 9. Tracking links

Every link to offladder.com carries UTM parameters, so the site can count three-question starts by series and by video.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| `utm_source` | `youtube` |
| `utm_medium` | `description`, `pinned-comment`, `channel` (profile links and banner) |
| `utm_campaign` | Series slug: `90-minutes`, `last-tuesday`, `unknown-job`, `change-one-thing`, `honest-answers`, `shorts` |
| `utm_content` | Video slug, for example `dont-know-what-career` |

**Example:**
`https://offladder.com/start?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=description&utm_campaign=honest-answers&utm_content=dont-know-what-career`

- Link to **`/start`** (the three questions) for product CTAs, and to **`/for-parents`** for parent CTAs. Use the homepage only for brand links.
- **Confirm with the site team** that analytics records UTM parameters on `/start` before launch.
- **Optional:** a short spoken path such as `offladder.com/yt` that redirects to a UTM-tagged `/start` link. It needs a redirect set up on the site before anyone says it on camera.

## 10. Shorts CTAs

- Links in Shorts descriptions and comments **aren't clickable** ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639)). Instead:
  - Attach the matching long-form video as the Short's **related video**. This is the main CTA.
  - Keep offladder.com in the **channel profile links**, where it is clickable.
  - Say *"three questions at offladder.com"* in no more than one Short in four. More than that and Shorts start to feel like ads.
- Put comment prompts in **on-screen text**, not voiceover, so the loop isn't broken.

---

## 11. Never

- Ask for a subscribe, like or click in the first minute.
- Put a product CTA before the payoff.
- Describe OffLadder as choosing a career, finding "your perfect job", or predicting the future.
- Mention prices on camera.
- Hide that OffLadder is ours.
- Say "link in bio".
- Recommend that under-16s contact strangers, spend money, or do anything that needs a trusted adult without saying so.
