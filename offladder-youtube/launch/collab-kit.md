# Collab kit: borrow an audience

*Written 30 September 2026. Use it with the [seed kit](seed-kit.md).*

An Instagram collab post has two authors. Once the invited creator accepts, the Reel appears on both profiles, is shown to both sets of followers, and its likes, comments and views are pooled. If they decline, it stays on ours only ([how collab posts work](https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-collaboration-post-how-to-collab-add-after-posting-more)). For an account with no followers, it's the fastest honest way to reach people.

Aim for **two or three creators by Wed 7 October**, so the writers episode and the next "Cooked or not?" episodes can go out as collabs.

## Who to ask

- **They make content about careers, study or a specific job** (day in the life, uni advice, "what I actually do at work").
- **Their audience is the one we choose on Mon 5 October:** students and recent graduates, or people changing career in their late 20s and 30s. Until then, ask both.
- **They're adults.** The channel is teen-safe and we don't approach creators under 18.
- **Their comments are real conversations,** and their follower count has grown steadily (a sudden jump can mean bought followers).
- **Their tone fits:** honest about AI, neither doom nor hype. No get-rich-quick, crypto or MLM content.
- **Size:** our starting range is 2,000 to 50,000 followers. Smaller creators are more likely to read a DM and say yes. This is our judgement, not data; the reviews will tell us if it's right.

## The message

Send it from @offladder, or from your own account if you know them. Keep it short and specific, and mention something real from their videos.

> Hi [name], I'm [your name] from OffLadder (@offladder). We make 30-second videos on whether jobs are "cooked" by AI, using only public data: Microsoft's ranking of 785 jobs, US Bureau of Labor Statistics projections and New York Fed graduate data.
>
> Your video on [something specific they posted] is exactly the kind of honest take our viewers want. Would you co-author one of our Reels on Instagram? It would be [the writers episode / an episode on your job], and it would appear on both our profiles. You can watch it before it goes out and say no at any point. No cost.
>
> Happy to send it over first.

**If they don't reply,** follow up once after four or five days, then leave it:
> Hi [name], just floating this back up in case it got buried. No worries at all if it's not for you.

**If they say yes:** send the video file and caption, and agree the date. Ask whether they'd like to add a line to the caption.

**If money or anything of value changes hands,** it's an ad. It needs the platform's paid partnership label on Instagram, the content disclosure on TikTok, and "ad" in the caption, as the ASA requires in the UK and the FTC in the US. Nothing in this kit involves payment.

## Adding them in Metricool

1. Open the scheduled Reel in Metricool and add the creator's Instagram handle as a collaborator. Through the API, it's `instagramData.collaborators: [{"username": "<handle>", "deleted": false}]` on the scheduled post.
2. When the Reel publishes, Instagram sends them the invite. It appears on their profile once they accept.
3. Log it below. After 48 hours, note the Reel's reach next to our average for Reels without a collaborator.

**TikTok** has no equivalent we can schedule, but Stitch and Duet are switched on for every video. Invite creators to stitch an episode with their own take.
