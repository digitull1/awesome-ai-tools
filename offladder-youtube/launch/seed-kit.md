# Seed kit: the first 100 real followers

*Written 30 September 2026, after the first performance check (see the [launch log](README.md)).*

On 30 September the accounts had no audience: 0 followers on Instagram and TikTok, one YouTube subscriber, and 4 people reached on Instagram in total. The posts aren't failing; hardly anyone has seen them yet. A new account's first viewers and engagers are its test audience, and they teach each app who the account is for. So the first 100 followers should be real people from the audience we want, starting with people who already know OffLadder.

**The accounts:** Instagram [@offladder](https://www.instagram.com/offladder/) · TikTok [@offladder](https://www.tiktok.com/@offladder) · YouTube [@offladder](https://www.youtube.com/@offladder)

**Target:** 100 real followers on Instagram and on TikTok by the review on Monday 12 October. Check progress at the day-7 review on Monday 5 October.

## Never do this

- **Don't buy followers, likes or views, and don't join engagement groups** ("pods", like-for-like threads, follow trains). Both platforms ban it. Meta's rules, which cover Instagram, prohibit "selling, buying, or exchanging for engagement, such as likes, shares, views, follows" ([Spam policy](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/spam/)). TikTok's ban on fake engagement covers trading followers and likes ([Community Guidelines, integrity and authenticity](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity)). It also teaches both apps the wrong audience, which is worse than a slow start.
- **Don't use OffLadder's user data** for any of this: no emails to users, no messages to people who signed up. Everything below uses public or personal channels.
- **One account per person.** No second accounts to comment or like.

## Team actions (10 minutes, today)

Everyone on the team, and anyone close to OffLadder who's happy to help:

1. **Follow** @offladder on Instagram and TikTok and subscribe on YouTube. Turn on post notifications for the first two weeks.
2. **At 18:00 UK on post days, watch the new post to the end.** Then like it and answer its question in the comments, honestly and in your own words. A real answer starts the thread; "great post" doesn't.
3. **Send the posts that fit to people who'd want them.** A private send (DM, WhatsApp, iMessage) is a strong signal on both apps: Instagram's own list of what it predicts for Reels starts with "how likely you are to reshare a reel" ([Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)). Aim for 10 sends each this week, starting with the POV chat.
4. **Reply to every comment within a few hours**, from @offladder. Where a post promises a reply (*"We'll reply with its rank out of 785"*), keep the promise: the lookup is in [lookup/](lookup/).
