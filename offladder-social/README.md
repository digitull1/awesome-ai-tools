# OffLadder Instagram media (do not merge)

These files are the images and videos for OffLadder's scheduled Instagram posts:

- `week1/`: Days 1 to 7 (4 to 10 October 2026)
- `weeks2-4/`: Days 8 to 29 (11 October to 1 November 2026), except the posts that need a guest, community replies or follower questions

They sit here so Metricool can fetch them by public link when the posts are scheduled. Metricool keeps its own copies, so this folder is not part of the awesome list and the branch can be deleted once the posts are scheduled.
